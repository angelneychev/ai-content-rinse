Screenshots are captures of the actual version 0.4.0 interface, cropped to the plugin content area or preview dialog. They use demonstration text and an image containing an explicitly added sample PNG text chunk. No results were fabricated or painted into the interface. Temporary post and media fixtures were removed after capture.

The gallery was expanded on September 14, 2026 at Angel's request. The first three images now follow the same demonstration article through settings, preview and applied changes. They show the editor in use rather than only an isolated preview dialog. The final three retain the paste, markup and media examples.

Artwork uses an ivory document, a mint water ribbon and a forest green background. Banners identify Angel Neychev as the author. No third-party brand marks are included. Artwork is distributed under the repository's GPL-2.0-or-later license.

Screenshot captions are in `readme.txt` in the same numbered order. The deployment action sends `.wordpress-org` to the top-level SVN `assets` directory after WordPress.org approval and configuration of SVN access. These images are excluded from the installed plugin and its ZIP.

Source: [WordPress.org asset requirements](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/plugin-assets/).
